# CricketStream 3.0 beta 5 — commentary, and staying on air

**Commentary from a phone, and a stream that looks after itself.** Anyone can commentate
from their own phone by scanning a code on the clubhouse screen. Behind the scenes, match
day now rides out a server that keeps crashing, OBS crashing or freezing, and the line
dropping, and the clubhouse screen shows how all of it is getting on.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `CricketStream.exe` (Arm laptops: `CricketStream-arm64.exe`) |
| **Mac** | `CricketStream-mac.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
| **Linux** | `CricketStream-linux` (for a scorer's agent or bridge) |
| **Clubhouse screen** | `CricketStream-clubhouse-pi.img.xz` (Raspberry Pi 4 or 5; flash with Raspberry Pi Imager) |

Already on an earlier beta? The app offers the update itself: one click, and it checks the
new version before replacing anything. A clubhouse screen on beta 4 updates itself the next
time it starts.

## What's new

- **Commentary from a phone.** A **Commentary** tab on the clubhouse screen: turn it on and
  a QR code comes up. Whoever scans it can ask to commentate from their phone, with nothing
  to install, and you choose who goes on air. Viewers see their name in the corner, and the
  ground's sound dips under their voice by itself. Volume and mute on the screen and the
  phone. See CLUBHOUSE_SCREEN.md.
- **A Health tab on the clubhouse screen.** How everything's getting on, worst first: the
  stream and its line, the scorer's feed, the graphics reaching OBS, OBS crashes, and the
  laptop slowing down; then what the last pre-match check found. It reads **Health ✗** when
  something's wrong.
- **A sharper win predictor.** It works through the match ball by ball, as the TV ones do,
  and knows how strong today's two sides are from their games this season, and, when the
  clubs have entered their XIs on PlayCricket, whether a side is stronger or weaker than
  usual. Tested on 484 Devon league matches, each predicted only from the games before it:
  before a ball is bowled it's no longer a coin toss, and it's at least as good as the old
  one at every stage of a chase.
- **A scorecard you can watch.** With YouTube signed in, the match page lists each innings'
  wickets and milestones, each a link to that moment in the match video.
- **New four and six graphics**, in the batting side's colours and each scorebar style's
  look.

## Staying on air

Each of these was found by deliberately breaking match day on a real OBS, and the whole set
was run again before this beta: the stream stayed up through every one.

- **The server keeps crashing?** Match day no longer gives up after three restarts: it
  tries once a minute until you stop it. The window, the clubhouse screen (**The graphics
  have stopped**) and the club's Discord say what's happening, and a replay on air is cut
  back to the live picture rather than left there.
- **Stuck on the replay.** If the server stopped mid-replay, OBS stayed on the Replay scene.
  It now cuts back by itself.
- **A burst of old graphics.** After a gap of more than a minute in the score, the first
  score back played every four, six and wicket it had missed, with replays of footage long
  gone. Now the scorebar just catches up.
- **Logged out after a crash.** With a club password, a crash restart logged the clubhouse
  screen out for the rest of the match. A login now lasts the match day.
- **A server that never answered** was never replaced, which also blocked OBS's crash
  recovery. It's now replaced after two minutes.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/BridestoweBelstoneCC/Cricket-Live-Stream/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Still a beta

- Commentary has been tried on an iPhone, over a club's home wifi. It hasn't been tried on
  an Android phone or on a ground's wifi yet, so rehearse it before you rely on it.
- Rehearse with **Match day → Practice match** before your first stream. The proven **v2.12**
  (the Python edition) stays available on the
  [`python` branch](https://github.com/BridestoweBelstoneCC/Cricket-Live-Stream/tree/python).

Found something? Tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/2nuBPj9rH7) or open an
[issue](https://github.com/BridestoweBelstoneCC/Cricket-Live-Stream/issues). The Activity
log (`logs/` beside the app) says what happened.
