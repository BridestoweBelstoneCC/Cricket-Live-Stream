# CricketStream 3.0 beta 4 — more from the ground

**More of the match on the stream.** A second camera now gives side-on wicket replays and
the walk out to the middle. In a rain delay, viewers see the rain radar and when it should
be dry. The stream celebrates club milestones, and the match video gets its own thumbnail
and a highlights video by the evening.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `CricketStream.exe` (Arm laptops: `CricketStream-arm64.exe`) |
| **Mac** | `CricketStream-mac.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
| **Linux** | `CricketStream-linux` (for a scorer's agent or bridge) |
| **Clubhouse screen** | `CricketStream-clubhouse-pi.img.xz` (Raspberry Pi 4 or 5; flash with Raspberry Pi Imager) |

Already on an earlier beta? The app offers the update itself: one click, and it checks the
new version before replacing anything.

## What's new

- **Try it with a practice match.** Starting match day now asks "How is today's match being
  scored?" with a third answer: **A practice match**. OBS opens as usual and a made-up
  match plays through the scorebar, replays and graphics, so you can check the whole set-up
  with no scorer. It's paced like real cricket, so each replay plays out before the next ball.
- **A QR code for the scorer's phone.** Start match day for scoring by hand and a QR code
  for the scoring page comes up, on the laptop and on the clubhouse screen. Scan it and
  close it; **Scoring link** brings it back.
- **More from a second camera** (optional; nothing changes with one camera): a wicket
  replays from camera 2 as well ("Side-on"; needs FFmpeg), camera 2 shows the new batter
  walking out and the teams walking off, and the over-change cut holds it as long as you set.
- **Rain radar.** In a rain delay, the last hour of rain around the ground and when it
  should be dry; before play or at a break when rain's forecast; and a warning strap half
  an hour before rain arrives. Free services, no key.
- **Club milestones.** A strap when a home player passes 500, 1,000… runs or 50, 100…
  wickets for the club, or makes a first fifty, a hundred or a five-for — added up from
  every past PlayCricket scorecard of the club's sides.
- **Season leaders** in the pre-match and break shows: the club's top three run-scorers and
  wicket-takers this season.
- **A thumbnail on the match video** (both badges, the teams, the result), and **a
  highlights video** uploaded after the match from its replays.
- **Fixed:** with manual scoring, the first ball could take up to 20 seconds to reach the
  scorebar.
- **Fixed:** finishing from the clubhouse screen left OBS's update check switched off.

The clubhouse screen was tested end to end on a real Raspberry Pi for this beta: pairing,
starting match day, the score and stream picture, and Finish.

The full list is in the
