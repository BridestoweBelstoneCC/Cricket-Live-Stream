# CricketStream 3.0 beta 14 — the cameras on the clubhouse screen

**The clubhouse screen's Cameras tab shows which camera is on air, and the cameras can have
their own names.** Nothing else has changed since beta 13;
[beta 12's notes](https://github.com/BridestoweBelstoneCC/Cricket-Live-Stream/releases/tag/v3.0.0-beta.12)
and [beta 11's](https://github.com/BridestoweBelstoneCC/Cricket-Live-Stream/releases/tag/v3.0.0-beta.11)
still apply.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `CricketStream.exe` (Arm laptops: `CricketStream-arm64.exe`) |
| **Mac** | `CricketStream-mac.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
| **Linux** | `CricketStream-linux` (for a scorer's agent or bridge) |
| **Clubhouse screen** | `CricketStream-clubhouse-pi.img.xz` (Raspberry Pi 4 or 5; flash with Raspberry Pi Imager) |

The laptop offers the update in its window: one click. A clubhouse screen on beta 13
follows the laptop by itself at the next quiet moment, or press **Update the screen** on
the laptop's match-day screen.

## New

- **The clubhouse screen lights the camera that's on air.** The Cameras tab's cut buttons
  show which camera is live, whoever cut to it: the screen, the control panel on a laptop or
  phone, or the automatic cut at the end of an over.
- **The cameras can be given their own names** ("Pavilion end"), with the screen's
  keyboard: when a found camera is added, or with **Rename the cameras** on the Cameras
  tab. Only the buttons change; OBS's own names for the cameras stay as they are.
