# CricketStream 3.0 beta 3 — a show around the cricket

**The stream has something on it when there's no cricket.** Before play there's a
countdown, the head to head and both teams' XIs. At drinks, the innings break or a rain
delay there's a break show with the best replays so far. After the stream the YouTube
video gets chapters for every wicket and milestone. Drinks breaks, which never really
showed, now work.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `CricketStream.exe` (Arm laptops: `CricketStream-arm64.exe`) |
| **Mac** | `CricketStream-mac.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
| **Linux** | `CricketStream-linux` (for a scorer's agent or bridge) |

Already on beta 1 or 2? The app offers the update itself: one click, and it checks the new
version before replacing anything.

## What's new

- **A pre-match show.** Go live before play and viewers see a countdown to the start
  ("Play starts at 13:00 · in 25 min"), the head to head with today's opposition, both
  teams' XIs from PlayCricket, and the ground's weather until the first ball.
- **Head to head.** The record of today's two teams over their last five meetings, and how
  the last one went. In the pre-match show, or any time from **On-screen extras → Show
  head-to-head**.
- **The break show, and drinks that work.** At drinks, the innings break, or a break you
  start from the control panel (**Breaks → Rain delay / Break in play**), the stream shows
  where the match stands, the scorecard and the weather, and replays the match's best
  moments so far. It cuts back the moment play restarts.
- **Chapters on the match video.** After the stream, the YouTube video gets a chapter for
  each innings, wicket, fifty, hundred and five-for, so viewers can jump straight to them.
- **Alerts on Discord.** The match-day alerts (the scorer's feed going quiet, OBS crashing,
  the stream stopping) can go to a Discord channel, so whoever's at the ground hears about
  them on their phone.
- **Clubhouse screen:** a ready-made Raspberry Pi image, pairing with one press on the
  laptop instead of typing a key, and score-only screens for the bar or the pavilion.
- **Fixed:** the league table graphic had no background.
