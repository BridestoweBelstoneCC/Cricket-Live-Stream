# CricketStream 3.0 beta 8 — replays after the animation, fielding records

**Fixes from the beta 7 practice match, and fielding in the club records.** A boundary's replay
now waits for its FOUR / SIX animation, and the scorebar between the innings shows the reply
about to start. If you're on an earlier beta, update before your next match.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `CricketStream.exe` (Arm laptops: `CricketStream-arm64.exe`) |
| **Mac** | `CricketStream-mac.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
| **Linux** | `CricketStream-linux` (for a scorer's agent or bridge) |
| **Clubhouse screen** | `CricketStream-clubhouse-pi.img.xz` (Raspberry Pi 4 or 5; flash with Raspberry Pi Imager) |

Already on an earlier beta? The app offers the update itself: one click, and it checks the
new version before replacing anything. A clubhouse screen updates itself the next time it
starts.

## What's changed

- **A four's or six's replay comes after its animation.** The replay's transition and banner
  used to come up on top of the FOUR / SIX animation, and a six could cut to the replay before
  its animation had finished. The clip is still saved at the same moment, so it shows the same
  footage. It just goes on air once the animation is over.
- **The scorebar between the innings shows the reply.** Until the reply's openers were named it
  showed the pre-match bar ("HOME — 0.0", P1). Now it's the chasing side at 0-0, P2, "Innings
  break" and what they need, in their colours.
- **Catches, stumpings and run outs in the club records.** Each player's fielding for the club
  now comes from the same PlayCricket scorecards as their runs and wickets. A catch that takes
  one of yours to 50, 100 or 150 for the club gets a club milestone strap ("Karl Jones: 50
  catches for the club").
- **Fixed: the scorer's laptop could say it had stopped sharing a moment before it had.**

## Still a beta

- The catch milestone reads NV Play's last-wicket fields, which haven't been seen on a real
  match's feed yet, so it may not appear on its first real chance. The club records' catch
  counts come from PlayCricket and don't depend on it.
- The replay timing and the new scorebar have been checked in tests and a browser, not yet on a
  real stream.
- If your scorer names the openers only as the first ball's bowled, a first-ball boundary can
  still be covered by graphics, though its replay still plays.
- The result assumes a side of eleven, so "won by N wickets" counts from ten wickets.
- Commentary hasn't been tried on an Android phone or on a ground's wifi.
- Rehearse with **Match day → Practice match** before your first stream. The proven **v2.12**
  (the Python edition) stays available on the
  [`python` branch](https://github.com/BridestoweBelstoneCC/Cricket-Live-Stream/tree/python).
