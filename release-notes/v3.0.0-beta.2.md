# CricketStream 3.0 beta 2 — for grounds with poor internet

**Auto quality now works, and steps back up.** The stream quality ladder in beta 1 didn't
reach OBS's encoder on match day, so a step down restarted the stream and changed nothing,
and it never stepped back up. Both are fixed, and tested against a real OBS on a throttled
line. Updating is recommended for every club, especially if your ground's internet comes
and goes.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `CricketStream.exe` (Arm laptops: `CricketStream-arm64.exe`) |
| **Mac** | `CricketStream-mac.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
| **Linux** | `CricketStream-linux` (for a scorer's agent or bridge) |

Already on beta 1? The app offers the update itself: one click, and it checks the new
version before replacing anything.

## What's new

- **Auto quality that really changes the quality.** When the line struggles, the stream
  steps down, to 480p at the lowest step. Once the line has been clean for 10 minutes, it
  climbs back one step at a time, only at a break in play and never during a replay. The
  Stream quality card shows what OBS is really sending. Auto mode is off by default:
  switch on **Auto quality scaling** in the Stream quality card, and stream with a stream
  key (with OBS signed in to YouTube, a quality change ends the broadcast). The pre-match
  check suggests it when your upload is tight.
- **Keeps going when things go wrong.**
  - If the server freezes, it's restarted, and auto quality carries on from the step it
    was on.
  - If OBS opened before the server was ready, its scorebar page is reloaded.
  - If OBS is slow to come back after a crash, CricketStream waits for it.
  - Last match's score stays off air until the scorer starts.
  - A replay asked for while the replay buffer is stopped is skipped, rather than showing
    the previous clip, and the buffer is started for the next one.
- **Mac:** the scorebar no longer goes missing when AirPlay Receiver is left on.
- **Clubhouse screen (optional):** a touch screen in the clubhouse showing the score,
  scorecard, overs and league table, which can switch the streaming laptop on and off.
- **Test the camera cut** before a match, and the **CPU warning now works on Windows**.
