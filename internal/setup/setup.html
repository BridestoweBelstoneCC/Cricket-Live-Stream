What is this computer for?

Pick the job this machine does on match day.

Club details

These appear on the scorebar and graphics.

PlayCricket

Your club ID is the number in your club's play-cricket.com address. The API key lets CricketStream fetch today's fixture, season stats and result cards.

Scoring software

The folder where NV Play / PCS Pro writes its scoreboard output. Leave it blank if the scorer is on another laptop (set that up from the control panel later) or if you'll score from a phone at /scoring.

The folder where NV Play / PCS Pro writes its scoreboard output on this machine.

Scorebar style

The look of the score strip along the bottom of the stream. All four use your club colours — pick on the feel, not the colour.

OBS and replays

Nothing to set up in OBS — CricketStream switches on its WebSocket server and replay buffer for you on match day. Replay clips are saved here.

Stream

YouTube Studio → Go Live → Stream settings. Key-based streaming survives restarts and quality changes; OBS's "connect account" mode ends the whole broadcast if the stream stops. Treat it like a password.

AI commentary (optional)

Powers live over commentary, match reports and social posts. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

Control panel access

The club password is what operators type to unlock the control panel. Left blank, the panel is open to anyone who can reach it.
